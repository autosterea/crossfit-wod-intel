2026 CrossFit Games Quarterfinals: Full Results & Recap

by Greg Patuto Published on Apr 15, 2026

This post may contain affiliate links (disclosure policy).

Austin Hatfield and Kyra Milligan were victorious in stage two of the season!

The second stage of the 2026 CrossFit Games took place from March 26-30. The quarterfinals featured thousands of athletes going through four different workouts in an effort to finish near the top of the leaderboard. In the end, Austin Hatfield and Kyra Milligan finished at the top of the standings.

Athletes were given five days to complete all four workouts and submit their times.
